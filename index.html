About

I am a PhD student in the Robot Learning Lab at Imperial College London, supervised by Prof. Edward Johns. My research focuses on robot learning for dexterous five-finger manipulation. I also co-lead Imperial’s team on GRAIL, a four-year EU Horizon-funded initiative to develop a general-purpose robotics foundation model.

Previously, I worked as a Research ML Engineer at Armnet and a Control Engineer Intern at Humanoid. I completed my MSc in Robotics and Artificial Intelligence at University College London with Distinction, where I conducted research on reinforcement learning for the Kangaroo robot under Prof. Valerio Modugno.

I completed my BSc in Artificial Intelligence, graduating with 110/110 cum laude, through the joint programme of the University of Pavia, the University of Milan, and the University of Milan-Bicocca.

Outside research, I enjoy running, travelling, and reading about history and technology.
