About Me

Hello! I'm currently a Research Machine Learning Engineer at Armnet (UK), where I previously worked as a Control Engineer Intern at Humanoid (UK). I completed a Master's in Robotics and Artificial Intelligence at UCL. As part of my studies, I worked as a research intern on a project focused on developing RL policies for the Kangaroo robot, supervised by Prof. Valerio Modugno in collaboration with PAL Robotics.

When I’m not working, you’ll often find me out running, travelling, or diving into books about history and technology. I value balance between focused research and staying active and I’m always curious to learn something new.
